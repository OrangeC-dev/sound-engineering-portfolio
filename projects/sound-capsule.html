About the Project

Sound Capsule is an interactive archive designed to preserve the acoustic identity of places. Through field recording, careful audio restoration and place-based storytelling, it invites listeners to explore environments through listening rather than only looking.

Preservation First

Archiving the sonic identity of places so each recording remains connected to memory, location and future listening.
